## 1. Test purpose
This suite verifies the published `operation` `dynamics_shallow_water_source_2d_tc4_forcing__apply` at `L0`: that the published forcing equals externally evaluated reference values on two fixed grids sharing no argument but the three extents; that it is the residual of the forced 2D shallow water equations for the prescribed flow of the Controlled Spec §3, at two times; that a zero low leaves the geostrophic background unforced; that the forcing translates with the low; that the channel along `y` (`along_axis=2`) is the axis exchange of the channel along `x`, case by case; that the forcing is mirrored with the flow along and across the channel; that the smallest valid grid, one cell, is accepted and evaluated; and the input guard for each clause of the Controlled Spec §4 — an invalid component count (`ncomp/=3`, below and above three), an extent at its boundary (`nx=0`, `ny=0`), an orientation that is neither `1` nor `2` (below and above), a non-positive radius (`R_low<=0`, at zero and below), and a non-positive channel length (`L_along<=0`, at zero and below) — together with the zero-filled `S` §4 requires on a rejected input of a state that has cells.

The symmetry pairs (`docs/TESTS.md` §Symmetry pairs) the forcing admits are four, and the suite carries each. The **translation** along the channel is the translation case, in both orientations. The **axis exchange** is carried by a transposed copy, at `along_axis=2`, of each of the eight base normal cases (§2): on the zero-low copy the forcing is judged zero directly, and on every other copy it is judged against its base through the exchange of the Controlled Spec §3 (the last paragraph), which reverses `f`, `dfdr` and `psi0`. The base cases are judged by the instruments of the paragraphs below, so the pairs carry each instrument into the `along_axis=2` orientation; the two finite-difference copies are judged by their own finite-difference residual as well. The two **mirrors** — along the channel and across it — are carried on the late finite-difference case and on its `_axis` copy, each with every argument that is odd under it reversed (§2), so each orientation runs once with a negative `c_tr`, `psi0` and `ubar`.

Two clauses of the Controlled Spec are stated there and not observed here. The clause that no element of the input arrays is read on a rejected input has no case: a read leaves no trace in the captured state. And the divergence term $\tilde h\,(\tilde u_x+\tilde v_y)$ of $F_h$ is zero at every cell of every case, because the prescribed flow of the Controlled Spec §3 has zero divergence, so its presence in the operation is not observable by any case of this suite — a model that omits the term produces the same `S` on every case here.

Two further properties of the Controlled Spec are stated there and not observed here. The ORDER of the guard is one: a model that evaluates the forcing on a rejected input and then overwrites `S` with zeros satisfies the eight abnormal cases whose rejected input is a non-positive `R_low` or `L_along`, because the overwritten values are all the captured state holds and the compiler flags the host passes (no floating-point trapping) let the intermediate division by `R_low^2` or by `L_along` produce a non-finite value without a fault. On the four cases whose `ncomp` is not three, such a model writes outside `S`, which is not a behaviour this suite defines, admits or refuses. What the abnormal cases judge is the published output and the flag, not when the guard ran. The identical cancellations of the Controlled Spec §3 are the other, and there are three, stated here for `along_axis=1`: $g\tilde h_x-f\tilde v$ is zero, $g\tilde h_y+f\tilde u$ is $f'\psi$, and the whole depth residual reduces to $F_h=\psi_x\,(f'\psi-f\,c_{tr})/g$ (for `along_axis=2` the first two exchange their values and $F_h=-\psi_y\,(f'\psi+f\,c_{tr})/g$), a form in which the background depth, the geostrophic relation and the divergence term do not appear at all. A model that writes any of the three in its reduced form differs from one that evaluates them as written by at most `1.5e-14` over the cases of this suite (measured `5.7e-15` and `1.42e-14` on the two finite-difference cases, `8.4e-15` on `l0_translation_tau`, `3.9e-15` on the first reference-values case, `8.9e-16` on the second, `4.2e-15` on the zero-low case and `1.8e-15` on `l0_min_extent`; the same on each `_axis` copy, and `1.42e-14` on the two mirror cases whose `cross_reversed` is `0` and `1.33e-14` on the two whose it is `1`) — so no case here separates them, which was checked against every bound of §6 and not only against the tolerance `1.0e-8`: the reduced form gives `4.7e-12` and `3.6e-12` on the two reference-values cases and on `l0_min_extent` `4.7e-12` against a tolerance of `1.0e-8`, `4.8e-8` and `5.5e-8` on the two finite-difference cases against `[1.0e-9, 1.0e-6]`, `0.0` on the zero-low case against `1.0e-8`, on the translation case and its copy a ratio of `1.0e-14` against `[1.0e-18, 1.0e-9]` with both maxima at `5.874` against `[5.0, 7.0]`, and at most `1.6e-16` on the axis-exchange pairs and `8.1e-16` on the four mirror pairs against `1.0e-12` — every bound of §6, each satisfied (the finite-difference and zero-low copies give the base's values). The difference is not zero: `g*(f*psi_x/g)` is not `f*psi_x` in binary floating point. The third has a consequence for `l0_zero_low_gives_zero_forcing_pass`: the two models that judgment names are models that form $\tilde h_y$ and get its geostrophic term wrong, and a model that never forms $\tilde h_y$ satisfies that judgment for any low, so what the judgment pins is the term's sign and presence in a model that carries it, not that the model carries it.

The §2 precondition that the background is geostrophic is a precondition on the caller's input and not a judgment of this suite: every case of this suite supplies a balanced background, and no case supplies an unbalanced one, so what the operation does with an unbalanced background is not observed.

The two instruments answer different questions, and neither answers the other's. Each finite-difference case compares the published forcing against a residual the host builds from the same closed form of $\psi$ that the Controlled Spec §3 states, so a reading of $\psi$ that is wrong in the same way on both sides is invisible to both of them: with `R_low` a tenth of its value, `psi0` halved or sign-reversed, `r_c` or `s_c0` displaced, or the Gaussian written as $e^{-r^2/(2R_{low}^2)}$, the residual stays at `5.6e-9` to `6.6e-8`, under the threshold. The two reference-values cases are what pin the shape of $\psi$, its parameters and the reading of the arguments they are supplied in: their expected arrays are declared inputs of the cases, evaluated outside this suite, and each of those six misreadings deviates from the first by at least `3.8`. The second case varies every scalar argument but the three extents `ncomp`, `nx`, `ny` and the orientation, and gives `dfdr` a value that differs from row to row, which the `beta`-plane background of every other case cannot. The same holds in the `along_axis=2` orientation: the two finite-difference copies there read the same closed form as the model, and what pins the low's shape and the reading of its arguments in that orientation is the axis-exchange pair of each reference-values case with its base, whose expected array is a declared input. The forcing of each transposed reference-values case deviates from the transposed expected array by `4.709e-12` and `3.642e-12`, the rounding of the stated values, and every `along_axis=2` defect named in §6 moves the pair by `0.66` or more, but for the geostrophic sign on the quiescent case, which has no background velocity for the term to carry.

The host-rendered driver calls the `operation` once per case at the case's own arguments (§2) and writes its logical result into `guard_pass`, which starts at `0` (§5). A result the `operation` never assigns therefore captures `0`, which every `guard_pass == 1` of a `pass` test refuses. On the abnormal cases `guard_pass == 0` holds for such an `operation` too, and `rejected_outputs_zero` is what separates it on every abnormal case that has cells, because `S` starts at `-999`. On a case with no cell the flag is all there is to judge. It is not established that the kernel evaluates a transformed case on its own path: a kernel that realises `along_axis=2` by exchanging the arguments, evaluating at `along_axis=1` and exchanging the result back captures the same `S` to round-off, which every judgment of §6 accepts. The Controlled Spec §6 forbids that form and no judgment of this suite separates it.

- **What the driver holds and binds.** The driver holds two states: `S` (`ncomp` × `nx` × `ny`), which holds the literal `-999` at every element before the call (`S` is an output the `operation` only writes, so an element it does not write captures `-999`), and `guard_pass`, the `operation`'s logical result. On the two extent cases and their copies no array of no cell can be listed, so `derived.<name>` is the number `0.0`, which fills the array of no cell, for each of `f`, `dfdr`, `ubar`, `dubar`, `hbar` and for the empty one of `x` and `y`; the other coordinate is its input list.
- The finite-difference reference `S_fd` is not a state: it is a host expression in the predicates of the two finite-difference tests (§5), evaluated from the closed forms and the §2 inputs alone, so no element of it comes from an output of the `operation`. It is evaluated on the two finite-difference cases and their two `_axis` copies, from the prescribed flow of the Controlled Spec §3 by the host's own closed forms: on the base cases with `h(x,y,t) = hbar(y) + f(y)*psi(x,y,t)/g`, `u(x,y,t) = ubar(y) - psi_y(x,y,t)` and `v(x,y,t) = psi_x(x,y,t)`, on the `_axis` copies with `h(x,y,t) = hbar(x) + f(x)*psi(x,y,t)/g`, `u(x,y,t) = -psi_y(x,y,t)` and `v(x,y,t) = ubar(x) + psi_x(x,y,t)` (`f`, `ubar`, `hbar` the profiles of that copy's background as the Axis-exchange bullet above defines them — `hbar` the base's closed form evaluated at `x` — and `psi` with the along-channel coordinate `y`), and the conservation-form fluxes `Fx = [h*u, h*u*u + g*h*h/2, h*u*v]` and `Fy = [h*v, h*u*v, h*v*v + g*h*h/2]`, the reference at the cell `(i,j)` is
  `F_fd = (U(t+dt) - U(t-dt))/(2*dt) + (Fx(x+d) - Fx(x-d))/(2*d) + (Fy(y+d) - Fy(y-d))/(2*d) - S_cor`, with `U = [h, h*u, h*v]`, the Coriolis source `S_cor = [0, f*h*v, -f*h*u]` at the cell, `d=50.0` m and `dt=1.0` s. The differences are evaluated at the shifted positions from the closed forms, not from an array, so no grid boundary enters. ## 5. Judged quantities
A test is judged by the host's predicates over the captures alone (`docs/TESTS.md` requirement 9). Each quantity below is named on the `quantities` line of the tests that judge it, and is evaluated from the state captured after the call in each of the test's target cases.

The state of a case, captured before and after the call, consists of `S` and `guard_pass`. `S` (`ncomp` × `nx` × `ny`) is the output argument of the `operation`, and holds `-999` before the call (§2). `guard_pass` is the `operation`'s logical result, which the driver writes into the state after the call as `1` when the call returned true and `0` when it returned false; it starts at `0`. A captured `0` in `S` is a zero the `operation` wrote, which is what the abnormal cases judge, and an element the `operation` left untouched captures `-999` and fails every judgment of §6 that reads it.

The pair judgments and the translation judgment read another case's state after its call, through `at('<base>')`. A deviation, a maximum or a norm over the state is a judged quantity, never a state variable. The expected array of each reference-values case and of `l0_min_extent` is a declared input of the case, stated in §2 as `inputs.expected_S`, so that a host-evaluated judgment compares the captured `S` to it element by element rather than through a sum, a minimum or a maximum, which a permutation of the elements would satisfy. Every judgment of §6 is a statement about the state and the §2 inputs.

- `guard_pass`: the captured flag. It is `== 1` on the cases of a `pass` test and `== 0` on the cases of an `xfail` test.
- `pair_residual` (of `axis_exchange` and `mirror` below): for a transformed case and one of its arrays `q`, `norm2(q - T(q_base)) / norm2(q_base)` over every element, where `q_base` is the same array of the base case captured after its run and `T` the case's transformation, named by the last suffix of its `case_id`: for `_axis` the two spatial axes transposed and the second and third components exchanged, for `_xmir` the array reversed along `i` and the sign of its second component reversed, for `_ymir` the array reversed along `j` and the sign of its third component reversed (`docs/TESTS.md` §Symmetry pairs states the expressions). The array is `S`.
- `reference_values`: on each reference-values case and on `l0_min_extent`, against that case's own expected array of §2, `max|S - S_expected|` over the components and cells.
- `fd_residual`: on each of the two finite-difference cases and their two `_axis` copies, against the case's own `S_fd`, `max|S - S_fd|` over the components and cells. `S_fd` is written in the predicate's `bind`, in this order, one expression for both orientations, with `L = inputs.constants.L_along`, `g = inputs.constants.g`, the inputs `low.*` and `background.*` by their last name, and `t = inputs.time.t`:
  - `ax = 2 - inputs.along_axis` (`1` on a base case, `0` on an `_axis` copy), `d = 50.0`, `dt = 1.0`, `xcell = xc*L_x/nx`, `ycell = yc*L_y/ny`, `f0 = 2*Omega*sin(theta0)`, `beta = 2*Omega*cos(theta0)/a`, `k_y = pi/L_r`.
  - At each of the seven positions `P` with `(X, Y, T)`: `c` `(xcell, ycell, t)`, `tp` `(xcell, ycell, t + dt)`, `tm` `(xcell, ycell, t - dt)`, `xp` `(xcell + d, ycell, t)`, `xm` `(xcell - d, ycell, t)`, `yp` `(xcell, ycell + d, t)`, `ym` `(xcell, ycell - d, t)`, the names, each suffixed `_P`: `s = ax*X + (1-ax)*Y`, `r = ax*Y + (1-ax)*X`, `xi = pi*(s - s_c0 - c_tr*T)/L`, `psi = psi0*exp(-((L/pi)**2*sin(xi)**2 + (r - r_c)**2)/R_low**2)`, `ps = -(L/(pi*R_low**2))*sin(2*xi)*psi`, `pr = -2*(r - r_c)/R_low**2*psi`, `f = rotation_sign*(f0 + beta*(r - L_r/2))`, `ub = jet_sign*u0*sin(k_y*r)**2`, `hb = gh0/g - (u0/g)*(f0*(r/2 - sin(2*k_y*r)/(4*k_y)) + beta*(r**2/4 - L_r*r/4 - (r - L_r/2)*sin(2*k_y*r)/(4*k_y) - (cos(2*k_y*r) - 1)/(8*k_y**2)))`, `h = hb + f*psi/g`, `u = ax*ub - (ax*pr + (1-ax)*ps)`, `v = (1-ax)*ub + ax*ps + (1-ax)*pr`.
  - `F_h = (h_tp - h_tm)/(2*dt) + (h_xp*u_xp - h_xm*u_xm)/(2*d) + (h_yp*v_yp - h_ym*v_ym)/(2*d)`.
  - `F_hu = (h_tp*u_tp - h_tm*u_tm)/(2*dt) + (h_xp*u_xp**2 + g*h_xp**2/2 - h_xm*u_xm**2 - g*h_xm**2/2)/(2*d) + (h_yp*u_yp*v_yp - h_ym*u_ym*v_ym)/(2*d) - f_c*h_c*v_c`.
  - `F_hv = (h_tp*v_tp - h_tm*v_tm)/(2*dt) + (h_xp*u_xp*v_xp - h_xm*u_xm*v_xm)/(2*d) + (h_yp*v_yp**2 + g*h_yp**2/2 - h_ym*v_ym**2 - g*h_ym**2/2)/(2*d) + f_c*h_c*u_c`.
  - `S_fd = (comp-1.5)*(comp-2.5)/2*F_h + (comp-0.5)*(2.5-comp)*F_hu + (comp-0.5)*(comp-1.5)/2*F_hv`, the three factors selecting the components `1`, `2`, `3`.
- `zero_low`: on the zero-low case and its `_axis` copy, `max|S|` over the components and cells.
- `translation`: on `l0_translation_tau` against `l0_translation` and on `l0_translation_tau_axis` against `l0_translation_axis`, the ratio `max|S - roll(S_base, 3)| / max|S_base|` over the components and cells, where `S_base` is the base's `S` read through `at('<base>')` and `roll` shifts the index along the channel by `+3` cyclically (`roll(a, 0, 3, 0)` at `along_axis=1`, `roll(a, 0, 0, 3)` at `along_axis=2`), together with `max|S|` on each of the four cases.
- `axis_exchange`: on the `_axis` copies of the two reference-values cases, `l0_min_extent`, the two finite-difference cases and the two translation cases, `pair_residual` against the base of `S`.
- `mirror`: on the four mirror cases, `pair_residual` of `S` against the base.
- `rejected_outputs_zero`: on an abnormal case that has cells, `max|S|` over the components and cells. It is `== 0` when every element of `S` equals `0` exactly, and the pre-call `-999` fails it.
- `cross_target_state_agreement`: this target's run compared with every other target's certified variant of this node, case by case (`docs/TESTS.md` §Cross-target judgment): for one output `q` captured after the run and the same output `q_ref` of the same case in the other variant, `max|q - q_ref| / max(max|q|, max|q_ref|, 1)` over every element. The host evaluates it from the captured state, against each other variant separately; on a target with no other certified variant it holds vacuously. It is outside the `quantities` line.

- `suite.pass_rule`: `pass` when all `test_id` are `pass` or `xfail`.
