## 1. Test purpose
This suite verifies the published `operation` `dynamics_shallow_water_source_2d_tc4_forcing__apply` at `L0`: that the published forcing equals externally evaluated reference values on two fixed grids sharing no argument but the three extents; that it is the residual of the forced 2D shallow water equations for the prescribed flow of the Controlled Spec §3, at two times; that a zero low leaves the geostrophic background unforced; that the forcing translates with the low; that the channel along `y` (`along_axis=2`) is the axis exchange of the channel along `x`, case by case; that the forcing is mirrored with the flow along and across the channel; and the input guard for each clause of the Controlled Spec §4 that a captured state can observe — an invalid component count (`ncomp/=3`, below and above three), an orientation that is neither `1` nor `2` (below and above), a non-positive radius (`R_low<=0`, at zero and below), and a non-positive channel length (`L_along<=0`, at zero and below) — together with the zero-filled `S` §4 requires on a rejected input.

The symmetry pairs (`docs/TESTS.md` §Symmetry pairs) the forcing admits are four, and the suite carries each. The **translation** along the channel is the translation case, in both orientations. The **axis exchange** is carried by a transposed copy, at `along_axis=2`, of every normal case the guard accepts: on the zero-low copy the forcing is judged zero directly, and on every other copy it is judged against its base through the exchange of the Controlled Spec §3 (the last paragraph), which reverses `f`, `dfdr` and `psi0`. The base cases are judged by the instruments of the paragraphs below, so the pairs carry each instrument into the `along_axis=2` orientation; the two finite-difference copies are judged by their own finite-difference residual as well. The two **mirrors** — along the channel ($x \to L_x - x$, $u \to -u$) and across it ($y \to L_y - y$, $v \to -v$) — are carried on the late finite-difference case, each with every argument that is odd under it reversed (§2).

Three clauses of the Controlled Spec are stated there and not observed here. The extent clauses `nx<1` / `ny<1` have no case: a state of zero extent has no snapshot the judgment could read. The clause that no element of the input arrays is read on a rejected input has none either: a read leaves no trace in the captured state. And the divergence term $\tilde h\,(\tilde u_x+\tilde v_y)$ of $F_h$ is zero at every cell of every case, because the prescribed flow of the Controlled Spec §3 has zero divergence, so its presence in the operation is not observable by any case of this suite — a model that omits the term produces the same `S` on every case here.

Two further properties of the Controlled Spec are stated there and not observed here. The ORDER of the guard is one: a model that evaluates the forcing on a rejected input and then overwrites `S` with zeros satisfies the four abnormal cases whose rejected input is a non-positive `R_low` or `L_along`, because the overwritten values are all the captured state holds and the compiler flags the host passes (`-std=<standard> -O2`, no floating-point trapping and no `-fcheck`) let the intermediate division by `R_low^2` or by `L_along` produce a non-finite value without a fault. On the two cases whose `ncomp` is not three, such a model writes outside `S`, which is not a behaviour this suite defines, admits or refuses. What the abnormal cases judge is the published output and the flag, not when the guard ran. The identical cancellations of the Controlled Spec §3 are the other, and there are three, stated here for `along_axis=1`: $g\tilde h_x-f\tilde v$ is zero, $g\tilde h_y+f\tilde u$ is $f'\psi$, and the whole depth residual reduces to $F_h=\psi_x\,(f'\psi-f\,c_{tr})/g$ (for `along_axis=2` the first two exchange their values and $F_h=-\psi_y\,(f'\psi+f\,c_{tr})/g$), a form in which the background depth, the geostrophic relation and the divergence term do not appear at all. A model that writes any of the three in its reduced form differs from one that evaluates them as written by at most `1.4e-14` over the cases of this suite (measured `1.07e-14` and `1.34e-14` on the two finite-difference cases, `8.4e-15` on the second call of the translation case, `3.9e-15` on the first reference-values case and `8.9e-16` on the second; the same on each `_axis` copy, and `1.34e-14` on each mirror case) — so no case here separates them, which was checked against every bound of §6 and not only against the tolerance `1.0e-8`: the reduced form gives `4.7e-12` and `3.6e-12` on the two reference-values cases against a tolerance of `1.0e-8`, `4.7e-8` and `5.4e-8` on the two finite-difference cases against `[1.0e-9, 1.0e-6]`, `0.0` on the zero-low case against `1.0e-8`, on the translation case and its copy a ratio of `1.0e-14` against `[1.0e-18, 1.0e-9]` with both maxima at `5.874` against `[5.0, 7.0]`, and at most `1.7e-16` on the pairs against `1.0e-12` — every bound of §6, each satisfied (the finite-difference and zero-low copies give the base's values). The difference is not zero: `g*(f*psi_x/g)` is not `f*psi_x` in binary floating point. The third has a consequence for `l0_zero_low_gives_zero_forcing_pass`: the two models that judgment names are models that form $\tilde h_y$ and get its geostrophic term wrong, and a model that never forms $\tilde h_y$ satisfies that judgment for any low, so what the judgment pins is the term's sign and presence in a model that carries it, not that the model carries it.

The §2 precondition that the background is geostrophic is a precondition on the caller's input and not a judgment of this suite: every case of this suite supplies a balanced background, and no case supplies an unbalanced one, so what the operation does with an unbalanced background is not observed.

The two instruments answer different questions, and neither answers the other's. Each finite-difference case compares the published forcing against a residual the checks module builds from the same closed form of $\psi$ that the Controlled Spec §3 states, so a reading of $\psi$ that is wrong in the same way on both sides is invisible to both of them: with `R_low` a tenth of its value, `psi0` halved or sign-reversed, `r_c` or `s_c0` displaced, or the Gaussian written as $e^{-r^2/(2R_{low}^2)}$, the residual stays at `5.6e-9` to `6.6e-8`, under the threshold. The two reference-values cases are what pin the shape of $\psi$, its parameters and the reading of the arguments they are supplied in: their expected arrays are declared inputs of the cases, evaluated outside this suite, and each of those six misreadings deviates from the first by at least `3.8`. The second case varies every scalar argument but the three extents `ncomp`, `nx`, `ny` and the orientation, and gives `dfdr` a value that differs from row to row, which the `beta`-plane background of every other case cannot. The same holds in the `along_axis=2` orientation: the two finite-difference copies there read the same closed form as the model, and what pins the low's shape and the reading of its arguments in that orientation is the axis-exchange pair of each reference-values case with its base, whose expected array is a declared input. The forcing of each transposed reference-values case deviates from the transposed expected array by `4.709e-12` and `3.641e-12`, the rounding of the stated values, and every `along_axis=2` defect named in §6 moves the pair by `0.66` or more.

The call itself is not established either: `S` is a state variable the checks module owns, and the finite-difference reference `S_fd` is computed by the checks module, so a checks module that fabricates both instead of calling the `operation` is not excluded here. The omission of the call is excluded on every one of the twenty-two cases, and not everywhere by the same mechanism. On the fourteen cases the guard accepts, the setup value `-1` of `guard_pass` excludes it, because each of their judgments requires `guard_pass = 1`. On the eight abnormal cases it does not: their `pass_when` asks for `input_guard` among the failed checks, and §5 makes `input_guard` fail at `-1` as well as at `0`, so `checks.guard_flag_rejected` is what distinguishes the two there and is required by each of those judgments. The array sentinel is a second, independent exclusion, on every case but the two translation cases, where a uniform sentinel in both arrays cancels in the numerator of the ratio §6 judges (`-999` minus `-999`); that judgment bounds the ratio from below and the maximum of each array instead.

## 5. Diagnostics contract
- Require outputting `checks.reference_values`, `checks.fd_residual`, `checks.zero_low`, `checks.translation`, `checks.axis_exchange`, `checks.mirror`, `checks.rejected_outputs_zero`, `checks.guard_flag_rejected`, and `checks.input_guard` in `diagnostics.json`.
- The state of a case, captured after setup and after the run, consists of `S` (`ncomp` × `nx` × `ny`, the array the `operation` wrote at the call of a case that makes one), `S_fd` (`3` × `nx` × `ny`, the finite-difference reference of §2, written at setup and not written again), `S_t0` and `S_tau` (`3` × `nx` × `ny` each, the arrays the `operation` wrote at the two calls of the translation case), and `guard_pass` (scalar: `-1` at setup, before the `operation` is called; `1` when the last call returned true; `0` when it returned false). Every element of `S`, `S_t0` and `S_tau` is set to the sentinel value `-999` at setup, and `S_fd` is set to the sentinel on every case but the two finite-difference cases and their two `_axis` copies, which are the only cases that build it. The sentinel is not a value the `operation` produces on any case of this suite, so a captured `0` in `S` is a zero the `operation` wrote, which is what the abnormal cases judge, and an array the `operation` left untouched captures `-999` and fails every judgment of §6 that reads it. `S` keeps the sentinel on the two translation cases, which write `S_t0` and `S_tau` instead, and `S_t0` / `S_tau` keep it on every other case; no judgment reads an array on a case that does not write it.
- `guard_pass` is set to `-1` at setup and to the value the `operation` returned at the call, recorded as `1` / `0` because a state variable is real-valued. The setup value `-1` is distinct from both values the `operation` can return, so a case whose `operation` was not called captures `-1`, which no judgment of §6 accepts. This does not establish that the `operation` was called: the flag is a state variable the checks module owns, so a checks module that writes `0` into it without calling the `operation` captures the same value as a rejected input. What the setup value excludes is the omission of the call, not the fabrication of its result; the fabrication of the whole judged state is outside what this suite can observe and is stated in §1.
- Only the two `checks.fd_residual` judgments read a capture point other than the one after the run: they read `S_fd` at its setup capture. The pair judgments read another case's capture after the run, the base case's, through `at('<base>')`; the checks module keeps the base's arrays from that case's run (`docs/workflow/CHECKS_MODULE_CONTRACT.md` §2, the accumulator ordering rule). Every other judgment of §6 reads the capture after the run, where the setup sentinel of an array the `operation` did not write fails it. No state variable is derived by the checks module after the run: a deviation, a maximum or a norm over these variables is a `checks.<id>` status, never a state variable. `S_fd` is derived before the run, from the §2 closed forms and the §2 inputs alone, and no element of it is read from an output of the `operation`. The expected array of each reference-values case is not a state variable at all: it is a declared input of the case, stated in §2, so that a host-evaluated judgment compares the captured `S` to it element by element rather than through a sum, a minimum or a maximum, which a permutation of the elements would satisfy. Every judgment of §6 is a statement about these variables and the §2 inputs.
- Each check is computed on the case named here and is `na` on every other case: `checks.reference_values` on each of the two reference-values cases, against that case's own expected array; `checks.fd_residual` on each of the two finite-difference cases and their two `_axis` copies, against that case's own `S_fd`; `checks.zero_low` on the zero-low case and its `_axis` copy; `checks.translation` on the translation case and its `_axis` copy; `checks.axis_exchange` on the `_axis` copies of the two reference-values cases, the two finite-difference cases and the translation case; `checks.mirror` on the two mirror cases; `checks.rejected_outputs_zero` on every abnormal case (`pass` when every element of `S` equals `0` exactly, `fail` otherwise, the setup sentinel included); `checks.guard_flag_rejected` on every abnormal case (`pass` when `guard_pass = 0`, `fail` when `guard_pass = -1` and when `guard_pass = 1`), which is the one judgment that separates the setup value from a returned false; `checks.input_guard` on every case (`pass` when `guard_pass = 1`, `fail` when `guard_pass = 0` and when `guard_pass = -1`).
- `pair_residual` of a transformed case and one of its arrays `q` is `norm2(q - T(q_base)) / norm2(q_base)` over every element, where `q_base` is the same array of the base case captured after its run and `T` the case's transformation: for an `_axis` case the two spatial axes transposed and the second and third components exchanged, for `_xmir` the array reversed along `i` and the sign of its second component reversed, for `_ymir` the array reversed along `j` and the sign of its third component reversed (`docs/TESTS.md` §Symmetry pairs states the expressions). The array is `S`, and on the translation copy each of `S_t0` and `S_tau`.
- `cross_target_state_agreement` compares this target's run with every other target's certified variant of this node, case by case (`docs/TESTS.md` §Cross-target judgment): for one output `q` captured after the run and the same output `q_ref` of the same case in the other variant, it is `max|q - q_ref| / max(max|q|, max|q_ref|, 1)` over every element. The host evaluates it from the captured state, against each other variant separately; it is not a `diagnostics.json` field, and on a target with no other certified variant it holds vacuously.

## 7. Pass/fail aggregation rules
- `per_test.pass_rule`: `pass` when the judgment expression is satisfied.
- `per_test.xfail_rule`: `xfail` when `xfail_condition` is true and `pass_when` is satisfied.
- `suite.pass_rule`: `pass` when all `test_id` are `pass` or `xfail`.
